## Introduction
This study guide is designed to facilitate group or individual study of the Recovery Dharma book. Each section includes:
- **Reading**: Assigned chapters or sections to read.
- **Inquiry Questions**: Reflective questions to deepen understanding and personal connection to the material.
- **Meditation Theme**: Suggested meditation practices or themes related to the chapter's content.

## Preface

### Reading
Read the Preface of the Recovery Dharma book to understand the origins, purpose, and foundational principles of the program. Pay attention to how Recovery Dharma integrates Buddhist teachings with addiction recovery. (Pages xi-xiii)

### Inquiry Questions
- What motivated the creation of Recovery Dharma?
- How does Recovery Dharma differ from traditional 12-step programs?
- In what ways does the Preface set the tone for the rest of the book?

### Meditation Theme
Reflect on the intention behind your recovery journey. Consider your motivations and what you hope to cultivate through practicing Recovery Dharma.

---

## What is Recovery Dharma?

### Reading
Read the chapter titled "What is Recovery Dharma?" focusing on the definition, goals, and core components of the program. Note the emphasis on mindfulness, community, and the Four Noble Truths. (Pages 1-12)

### Inquiry Questions
- How is recovery defined in the context of Recovery Dharma?
- What are the core principles that underpin the program?
- How does mindfulness play a role in this recovery model?

### Meditation Theme
Practice mindfulness meditation, focusing on present-moment awareness and openness to experience as foundational aspects of recovery.

---

## Where to Begin

### Reading
Read the chapter "Where to Begin" which introduces the foundational practices and mindset for starting the Recovery Dharma path. Focus on the importance of honesty, willingness, and community support as the first steps. (Pages 13-22)

### Inquiry Questions
- What does it mean to be honest with yourself in recovery?
- How can willingness influence your recovery journey?
- Why is community support emphasized as a critical element for beginning?

### Meditation Theme
Meditate on the intention to be honest and open with yourself. Cultivate willingness to face challenges with courage and compassion.

## The Practice

### Awakening (Buddha)

#### Reading
Read the chapter "Awakening (Buddha)" which covers the historical Buddha’s life and awakening experience, and how these teachings relate to recovery. Pay attention to the Four Noble Truths and the Eightfold Path as a guide for personal transformation. (Pages 23-42)

#### Inquiry Questions
- What aspects of the Buddha’s awakening resonate with your own recovery experience?
- How do the Four Noble Truths apply to addiction and recovery?
- What steps of the Eightfold Path can you begin to practice in your daily life?

#### Meditation Theme
Practice mindfulness of breath meditation to cultivate awareness and presence. Reflect on the nature of suffering and the possibility of liberation.

### The Truth (Dharma)

#### Reading
Read the chapter "The Truth (Dharma)" which explores the teachings of the Buddha as they relate to understanding reality, suffering, and the path to freedom. Focus on the Four Noble Truths in depth and how these truths inform a practical approach to recovery. (Pages 43-60)

#### Inquiry Questions
- How do the Four Noble Truths deepen your understanding of addiction and recovery?
- In what ways can embracing the Dharma support your healing process?
- What insights from this chapter can you apply to your daily practice?

#### Meditation Theme
Engage in loving-kindness (metta) meditation to cultivate compassion for yourself and others, fostering acceptance and healing on the recovery path.
